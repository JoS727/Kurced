THE KURCED JOURNAL / DEBUT ALBUM / SEPTEMBER 2026

THE
COUNTDOWN.

Ten notes from the beautiful, unstable part of the process. The album is coming.

01 / THE ANNOUNCEMENT

I HAVE BEEN KEEPING A SECRET.

It is finally real enough to say out loud: my first album is coming this September. I keep writing that sentence and staring at it like it might disappear. It won't. I made something from every bad decision, every late-night spiral, every feeling I thought was too much. Now it gets to belong to you too.

I'm scared. I'm thrilled. I have never felt more alive.

02 / THE FEELING

I CAN'T SIT STILL.

There is a version of me that is trying to be calm about all of this. She is doing a terrible job. Every time I hear a piece of the album, I want to call everyone I know and make them listen immediately.

September feels impossibly far away and also like it is racing toward me. Either way: I am so ready.

03 / THE STUDIO

WE MADE A MESS.

The best kind. The kind with cables everywhere, lipstick on a coffee cup, notes on the walls, and a chorus that refuses to leave your body. I wanted this record to feel like a beautiful disaster you choose to walk into.

Some songs broke me open. Some songs put me back together with glitter and duct tape. All of them are mine.

04 / THE SONGS

THEY KNOW TOO MUCH.

I used to think I had to make myself smaller to be understood. This album is the opposite of that. It is loud where I was quiet. It is honest where I used to hide. It knows exactly what happened, even when I pretended it didn't.

I cannot wait for you to find the lyric that feels like it was written specifically for you.

05 / THE VISUALS

PRETTY CAN BE DANGEROUS.

I have been building the visual world around these songs like a diary you are not supposed to read. Dark rooms. Flash photography. Violet light. The exact moment after a bad idea becomes a great story.

Every image is a warning. Every image is an invitation.

06 / THE PEOPLE

I DIDN'T MAKE THIS ALONE.

There are people who saw the earliest, strangest versions of these songs and told me to keep going. They gave this record patience, honesty, volume, and a place to land when I thought I had nothing left.

I am so grateful I could scream. Maybe I already have.

07 / THE FANS

YOU MADE ME BRAVER.

If you have ever sent me a message, played a song twice, shared a clip, or simply believed there was more coming—I felt it. You helped me keep the door open long enough to walk through it.

This first album is mine, but it is also for every person who has ever felt too much and called it a flaw.

08 / THE COUNTDOWN

SEPTEMBER IS CALLING.

I can feel the countdown in everything now. In every draft. Every rehearsal. Every moment I almost reveal more than I should. The debut album is getting closer, and I am trying very hard not to leak the entire thing from pure excitement.

Stay close. The first warning is coming soon.

09 / THE PROMISE

I'M NOT PLAYING IT SAFE.

I didn't make this record to be background noise. I made it for car rides after midnight, mirrors before going out, headphones under blankets, and every moment you need something to say what you cannot.

It is dramatic. It is tender. It is a little cursed. Just like me.

10 / THE BEGINNING

THIS IS ONLY THE FIRST CHAPTER.

My first album arrives in September 2026. That sentence still makes my heart beat too fast. I hope you are ready to fall into this world with me.

Thank you for being here before the beginning even begins.
